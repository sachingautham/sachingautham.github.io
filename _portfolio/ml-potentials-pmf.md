Predicting how polymer-grafted nanoparticles interact is central to designing nanocomposite materials, but computing the potential of mean force (PMF) directly from molecular dynamics is expensive. I trained deep learning models on MD-simulated configurations to predict the effective PMF between two polymer-grafted nanoparticles, giving a fast, accurate surrogate for the underlying atomistic interactions.

This work is published as [Deep Learning Potential of Mean Force Between Polymer Grafted Nanoparticles](/publication/2022-deep-learning-pmf) in *Soft Matter* (2022).
