How does the sequence of monomers along a copolymer chain dictate its 3D structure? I combined evolutionary computing with coarse-grained molecular dynamics (CGMD) to efficiently screen the sequence space of a model AB-type copolymer, mapping the Pareto front of structure-space (e.g. radius of gyration) as a function of composition — work aimed at rational, sequence-specific control of macromolecular structure.

This work is published as [Sequence-Defined Pareto Frontier of a Copolymer Structure](/publication/2022-copolymer-pareto-frontier) in the *Journal of Polymer Science* (2022).
